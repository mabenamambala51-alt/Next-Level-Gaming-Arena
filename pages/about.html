About Next Level Gaming Arena

Next Level Gaming Arena is a premium gaming and indoor sports destination built for players who want energy, competition and community in one place. Since opening in 2023, we have become a hub for esports fans, casual gamers, sports teams and families looking for an exciting venue in Johannesburg.

OUR STORY

More than a gaming venue

At Next Level, we believe gaming is more than just playing — it is about connection, adrenaline and unforgettable experiences. Our arena was created to bring together next-generation gaming technology, competitive spirit and active indoor sports culture in a space designed for fun and performance.

Whether you're here for a quick match, a weekend tournament, a team challenge or a social event, Next Level is built to deliver a world-class experience with the heart of a community hub.
